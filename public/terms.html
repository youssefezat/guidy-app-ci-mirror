What Guidy is

Guidy helps you plan trips across Greater Cairo's public transport network: it finds routes, stops and fares, and — while you are actively riding a leg of a trip — shows other riders on the same route roughly where the vehicle is, based on phones already on board. Guidy is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated on behalf of the Egyptian National Authority for Tunnels, Cairo Metro, the Cairo Transport Authority, or any bus, microbus or rail operator. We aggregate and present transit information; we do not operate any vehicle, station or service shown in the app.

The app is free to use. It's supported by ads (see "Advertising" below); there is nothing to buy in the app itself, and we don't sell subscriptions or in-app items.

Accuracy of transit information

Please read this section before relying on the app for a trip you can't afford to get wrong.

Guidy has no live feed from any transit operator. Fares, lines, stops, schedules and frequencies come from published sources and from riders like you correcting them over time, and they can be wrong, incomplete, or out of date when an operator changes something without our knowledge. The live position shown for a vehicle you're waiting for is likewise an estimate built from other passengers' phones, not GPS hardware on the vehicle — it can be delayed, missing, or absent if no one else on that vehicle currently has the app open and riding.

Guidy is a planning aid, not a guarantee. Always allow extra time for anything time-sensitive — a flight, an exam, an appointment — and treat fares and schedules shown in the app as estimates to confirm with the operator, a posted sign, or the conductor when it matters. We are not responsible for missed connections, fares that differ from what's shown, or any loss arising from relying on information in the app.
